## Growth
1. **ANCHOR FACT.** Horizon West (southwest Orange County, north and west of Walt Disney World) had about 14,000 people in the 2010 census and 58,101 in the 2020 census. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horizon_West,_Florida (Census QuickFacts: https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/horizonwestcdpflorida/PST045223)
2. Lake Nona grew from about 1,500 residents in 2000 to about 64,000 by 2018; it is inside Orlando city limits, southeast of Orlando International Airport, and home to the UCF College of Medicine. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Nona,_Orlando,_Florida
3. Orange County: 1,429,908 in the 2020 census, estimated 1,528,002 in 2025; county seat Orlando; borders Seminole (north) and Osceola (south). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_County,_Florida
4. City of Orlando 307,573 (2020); metro 2,673,376, third largest in Florida. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando,_Florida (not used on page)

## Geography
5. SR 417 is about 54 miles long, runs through Osceola, Orange and Seminole counties, from I-4 in Celebration to I-4/SR 429 in Sanford. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_State_Road_417
6. SR 408 (East-West Expressway) runs about 22 miles from Florida's Turnpike in Gotha through downtown Orlando to SR 50 near UCF. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_State_Road_408
7. I-4 Ultimate rebuilt a 21-mile stretch of I-4 through Orlando (Orange and Seminole counties); express lanes opened February 26, 2022. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_4
