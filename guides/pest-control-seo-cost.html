What do agencies charge for local SEO?

A December 2024 study by SE Ranking and Duda collected monthly prices from 260 agencies, and 94% of those agencies serve small or local businesses.

Ahrefs found that providers who sell to local businesses charge $1,557 a month on average. That figure comes from a poll of 439 SEO providers, updated in August 2024. Ahrefs assumed every provider charges the top of its price band, so the average runs high. For work billed by the hour, Ahrefs puts the average rate at $111.

How many reviews does it take to reach the top three?

We ran three map searches for "pest control phoenix az" on November 10, 2026. They used the results that show up on a phone in downtown, north Phoenix and Ahwatukee.

No company made the top three in more than one of the three searches. In north Phoenix, every company in the top 10 had at least 110 reviews. The two downtown listings with 3 and 2 reviews didn't make the top 10 from north Phoenix or Ahwatukee.

Google says it ranks local results on three things: how well a profile matches the search, how close the business is, and how well known it is. Both top downtown listings had downtown addresses, so distance explains part of what we saw there.

More questions about cost

Is a Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Google charges nothing to create or keep a Business Profile. An agency's fee pays for the time it takes to run it each month.

Is there a setup fee?

No. The full $1,750 rebuild is included. If you'd rather run the profile yourself, the one-time Google Business Profile rebuild is $1,750 once.

Can I do this myself?

Yes. Each month that means posts, photos and new listings, plus asking customers for reviews. You'll also want a listing tool and a rank tracker, and both charge monthly fees.

How long before I see a change on the map?

Most profiles move on the map within 60 to 90 days, so start about three months before your busiest months.

Should I stop my Google Ads?

There's no need to. Ads can bring calls this week while the profile work builds. Your monthly report shows the calls, website clicks and direction requests from your profile. After a few months, you can compare those with what your ads bring in.
