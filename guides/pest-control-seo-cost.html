More questions about cost

Is a Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Google charges nothing to create or keep a Business Profile. An agency's fee pays for the time it takes to run it each month.

Is there a setup fee?

No. The full $1,750 rebuild is included. If you'd rather run the profile yourself, the one-time Google Business Profile rebuild is $1,750 once.

Can I do this myself?

Yes. Each month that means posts, photos and new listings, plus asking customers for reviews. You'll also want a listing tool and a rank tracker, and both charge monthly fees.

How long before I see a change on the map?

Most profiles move on the map within 60 to 90 days, so start about three months before your busiest months.

Should I stop my Google Ads?

There's no need to. Ads can bring calls this week while the profile work builds. Your monthly report shows the calls, website clicks and direction requests from your profile. After a few months, you can compare those with what your ads bring in.
