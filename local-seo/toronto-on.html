Questions Toronto business owners ask us

Do I need a Toronto address to show up on the map?

You can show up without one, but it helps to have one. Your Google profile needs the address where you meet customers, or a service area if you go to them. The map favours businesses near the person searching. Google removes profiles with made-up addresses, and we never use one.

Can you promise my business will make the top three?

No. Google decides rankings, and no agency controls them. Your monthly report shows where your profile ranks each month.

Do you work with my competitors?

We take one business per industry per area. Your free report tells you whether we already work with one near you.

How is this different from Google Ads?

Ads charge for every click and stop when the budget runs out. Google doesn't charge for clicks on your free profile listing. Profile work takes 60 to 90 days to show. You may want to keep your ads running while it builds.

What is Google Business Profile optimization?

It means making your Google profile complete and correct, so Google can match it to more searches. That covers your categories, services, description, photos, hours and service area.

How do I get more Google reviews?

A direct link to your review form makes it easy. Our free review link tool makes one, with a QR code. Then ask every customer after a good job.

Do you work with businesses outside Toronto?

Yes. We work with local businesses across Canada and the United States.
