How do you compare two SEO packages?

Ask each agency the same six questions.

  • Which currency? Ask whether the price is in Canadian or US dollars. We bill in US dollars.
  • What gets done each month? Ask for counts: listings, posts, photos, pages or hours.
  • Is Google Maps work priced on its own? A package that bundles a website, ads and social media can hide how little goes to your Google profile.
  • How long is the contract? Ask about the notice to cancel and any setup fee.
  • Who owns the work? Your profile, listings and website pages should stay yours if you leave.
  • Do they promise a ranking? No agency controls Google's rankings, so don't hire one that promises a spot.
