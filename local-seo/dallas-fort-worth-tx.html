Questions Dallas and Fort Worth business owners ask us

Do I need a Dallas or Fort Worth address to show up on the map?

You don't need a Dallas or Fort Worth one, but you do need an address or a service area. List the address where you meet customers, or a service area if you drive to them. Google removes profiles with made-up addresses, and we never use one.

Do I need one profile or two if I serve both cities?

A profile belongs to one business address. With one office you have one profile, and it can list the areas you serve. With an office in each city, each office gets its own profile, and each one is its own location with us.

Can you promise my business will make the top three?

No. Google sets rankings, and no agency can promise a position. We send a monthly report on where your profile ranks.

Do you work with my competitors?

We take one business per industry per area. The free report says whether we already work with one near you.

How is this different from Google Ads?

Ads charge for every click. Clicks on your profile listing are free, but a profile takes 60 to 90 days to show, so keep any ads on until then.
