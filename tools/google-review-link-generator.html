Google review link generator

Paste your Place ID to get a link that opens your Google review box, so customers don't have to search for you. If you already have the review link from your Business Profile, paste that instead. Either way, you also get a QR code to download and a card to print, free and without signing up.

In your Business Profile, the Ask for reviews button gives you a g.page review link. Google's Place ID Finder gives you the Place ID. A Google Maps link also works if it has the Place ID in it.

    Card to print

    How did we do?

    Point your phone's camera here to leave us a Google review.

    How should you ask customers for reviews?

    • Ask every customer. Google doesn't let a business pick who gets the link, so a customer you think is unhappy gets it too.

    • Give nothing in return. Google's rules ban discounts, gift cards, free services or any other reward for a review.

    • Send it by text after each job. The customer can tap the link on their phone right then.

    Source for the first two: Google's rules for reviews on Maps (prohibited and restricted content).

    Questions about review links

    Where do I find my Place ID?

    Open Google's Place ID Finder and type your business name into the search bar on the map. Pick your business from the list, and the ID shows up under its name. Paste it at the top of this page.

    Does it work if we drive to our customers?

    Yes. A business that serves customers at their homes and hides its address still has a review box on Google. The easiest way in is the Ask for reviews link in your Business Profile, which works in this tool too.

    Will the link stop working?

    It can, because Google sometimes changes a Place ID when it updates its map data. Open your link now and then to check it. If it ever opens the wrong business or an error, make a new link here and print a new card.
