What to fix on a roofing profile first

Google says local results are based mainly on three things: how well your profile matches the search, how close you are to the searcher, and how well known you are. Distance depends on where each searcher stands, so you work on the other two.

Your main category

Set it to Roofing contractor. If you clean gutters often, add Gutter cleaning service as a second category.

Address or service area

Google's rules don't allow a mailbox or a virtual office as the address, and Google can suspend a profile that uses one. If customers don't come to your office, the rules say to hide the address and set a service area. Draw that area around the towns your crews drive to.

The services to list

List roof repair and roof replacement as separate services, each with its own short description, since people search for each by name. If you do insurance claim work or emergency tarping, add those as services too. Leave off work you take on once or twice a year. The profile also has a short business description, where you can say what you do and which towns you cover.

Photos

Take photos on your own roofing jobs and add a few each time you finish one. If you're on our plan, you send them to us and we add 4 a month to your profile.

Reviews

Google's rules ban fake reviews and paid reviews. Ask every customer for one, and send a direct link to your review form. Our free review link tool makes that link, plus a QR code you can print on an invoice.

Your website

Give each main service its own page and link your profile to the one that fits best. Each service page should list the areas you cover. Use the phone number your office answers as the main number on the profile and the site. Google says the business must control that number.

Sources: Google Business Profile Help, “Tips to improve your local ranking on Google” and “Guidelines for representing your business on Google.”

A ten-minute check of your own profile

  • Search for “roofer near me” on your phone from a recent job site, and write down where your company shows up.

  • Open your profile and check that the main category reads Roofing contractor.

  • Make sure repair and replacement show as two separate services.

  • Count the photos you added in the last 90 days.

  • Read your last five reviews and note the month each one came in.

  • Open the Performance tab and write down last month's calls, website clicks and direction requests.
