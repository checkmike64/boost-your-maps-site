What do people search for when they need a plumber?

Most people type "plumber" or "plumber near me." Many others type the job they need done. We pulled 15 plumbing searches from Google's Keyword Planner for people in Houston, and we found drain cleaning was the job searched most often.

Google tries to match each search to a business that does that job. So the jobs you list on your profile and your website decide which of these searches you can show up for. If your profile only lists "plumbing," Google has less to match when someone searches for drain cleaning or water heater repair.

When do plumber searches peak?

In the three Sun Belt cities, we found searches for "plumber" ran highest from July to October and lowest from November to March.

Cold snaps bring their own spike. In Houston, searches for "frozen pipes" jumped to 320 in January 2026, against 10 to 40 in every other month of that year. Dallas went to 210 that January. Nobody can plan for one of those weeks, so your hours, phone number and services need to be right before it comes.

Profile work takes 60 to 90 days to show. If your busy months are July to October, the time to start is spring.

What to fix first on a plumbing company's Google profile

These eight changes come in the order we make them on a plumbing profile. Each one follows Google's rules for business profiles.

  1. 1. Make "Plumber" your main category. Then add other categories only for work you do every week, and only when Google has a category that fits.
  2. 2. List each job as its own service. Use the words people search for, like drain cleaning, water heater repair and sewer line repair. Give each one a short description.
  3. 3. Hide your address if customers never come to you. Google asks businesses that go to the customer to set a service area instead, covering the towns you drive to.
  4. 4. Show the hours you pick up the phone. Mark your company as open 24 hours only if someone answers at night, so the people who call at 2 a.m. reach a person.
  5. 5. Add photos of your own jobs each month. Your trucks, your team and the work you finished show people who you are before they call.
  6. 6. Ask every customer for a review. Send a direct link to your review form after each job. Our free review link tool makes one, with a QR code. Google does not allow discounts or gifts in return for reviews.
  7. 7. Use the same name, address and phone number everywhere. Directories, maps apps and your website should match your profile, so Google can tell all of them are the same company.
  8. 8. Point your website button at the right page. Build a page for each main job you want more of, and link the profile to the page that matches its main category.

Sources for steps 1, 3, 6 and 7: Google, "Guidelines for representing your business on Google" and the Google Maps user-contributed content policy.

How do you find the best plumbing SEO company for you?

Ask to see a monthly report from a plumber they work with now. A good report shows the calls, website clicks and direction requests from the profile. It also shows where the profile ranks in each part of the service area. A report with only traffic charts can't tell you if the phone rang more.

Ask which jobs they will add to your profile and your website. For a plumber, local SEO is mostly profile optimization plus a page for each job you want more of. So the answer should name drain cleaning, water heaters or the work you do most. The organic results below the map come from your website pages, so those pages help too.

Ask if they work with other plumbers in your area. Ask who owns the profile if you leave, and how much notice you need to give. Walk away from anyone who promises a spot in the top three, because Google sets that order.
