What to fix first on a plumbing company's Google profile

Use this as a checklist for your profile. If we manage it, we set up the profile and add the listings, posts and photos each month. You keep your hours right, ask customers for reviews, take photos on your jobs for us to add, and look after your website.

  1. 1. Make "Plumber" your main category. Google asks you to use as few categories as you can to describe your core business. Add another only for work that is a main part of what you do.
  2. 2. List each job as its own service. Use the words people search for, like drain cleaning, water heater repair and sewer line repair. Give each one a short description.
  3. 3. Hide your address if customers never come to you. Google asks businesses that travel to customers to set a service area instead, made up of the towns you drive to.
  4. 4. Show the hours you answer the phone. Mark your company open 24 hours only if someone picks up at night.
  5. 5. Add photos of your own jobs each month. Take them on site: your trucks, your crew and the finished work.
  6. 6. Ask every customer for a review. Send a direct link to your review form after each job. Google does not allow discounts or gifts in return for reviews.
  7. 7. Use the same name and phone number everywhere. Google asks that the name on your profile match the one on your website, your signs and your paperwork. Your listings on directories and maps apps should show the same address too, but only if your profile shows one.
  8. 8. Point your website button at the right page. You or your web person can build a page for each main job you want more of. Link the profile to the page that matches its main category.

Sources: Google, "Guidelines for representing your business on Google" (steps 1 and 3, and the business name in step 7) and the Google Maps user-contributed content policy (step 6). Matching searches: Google Business Profile Help, "Tips to improve your local ranking on Google."
