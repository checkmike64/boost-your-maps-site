Changes to make on the Google profile

Google's help page names three things behind map results: how well a profile matches the search, how close the business is to the person searching, and how well known it is.

  • Categories. HVAC contractor, or the closest match, as the main category. Add air conditioning repair service and furnace repair service if you do that work.

  • Services. Furnace repair, AC repair, installs and maintenance, each listed on its own with a two-line description.

  • Service area. The towns or postal codes your trucks go to. If customers never come to your shop, Google asks you to hide the street address.

  • Hours. Show 24-hour service only if someone answers at night, and set holiday hours ahead of each holiday.

  • Posts and photos. Furnace tune-up posts in the fall and AC check posts in the spring, with photos of your own trucks and crews.

  • Reviews. Send a direct review link after each job. Our free review link tool makes one with a QR code.

Pages on your own website

Your profile has room for one website link. The pages behind it are yours to write, or your web person's. Point the link at your main heating and cooling page. From there, link to a page for each big job, like furnace repair and AC repair, and list the towns you serve on each one. When we set up your profile, we add a tracking tag to that link so the monthly report can count visits from the map.

When to start

In Toronto, furnace repair searches start to rise in October and jump in November. Make your changes by September to be set for that. AC repair searches jump from April to May, so the AC side should be done by February or March. In Phoenix the AC jump comes from February to March, so do that work in December or January.
