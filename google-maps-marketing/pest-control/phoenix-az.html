What Phoenix pest control reviews say

We read the 20 newest Google reviews of three companies, one from each search. They were ACTION Termite & Pest Control from City Hall, Blue Sky Pest Control from north Phoenix and Rove Pest Control from Ahwatukee. Of those 60 reviews, 56 gave five stars.

In the week before our search, from November 4 to 10, Blue Sky got 11 new reviews and ACTION got 1. Rove's newest review is from December 2025.

In 16 of ACTION's 20 reviews, the customer mentions the technician by name. That happened in 7 of Rove's 20 and 5 of Blue Sky's 20. ACTION answered 19 of its 20 reviews and Rove answered 16. Blue Sky hadn't answered any of its 11 new ones yet, all less than a week old.

Google lets reviewers tag what they paid, and 22 of the 60 reviews have a price tag. Of those, 9 price tags say $40 to $80, 7 fall between $80 and $180, and 6 say $200 or more.

Termites come up in 12 of the 60 reviews. Termite jobs had all 6 of the $200 tags and the only two reviews below 3 stars. One of those customers said they were charged for a second treatment after the warranty ran out. The other said the termite stations were set too deep.

Source: the 20 newest Google reviews of each company, read November 10, 2026.

Questions pest control owners ask us

We're already on Google Maps. Why aren't we in the top three?

When we searched from City Hall, the top two spots went to the two lead-gen listings. Both give downtown Phoenix addresses. Neither one made the top 10 in north Phoenix or Ahwatukee. Google weighs how close a business is to the person searching. So a company across town can miss the top three downtown. Your free report runs the search from your part of town.

Do we need thousands of reviews to make the top three?

In Ahwatukee, Rove Pest Control came first with 422 reviews, and only one other pest control company in that top 10 had any. In north Phoenix, the top three had 658 to 3,603 reviews, and every company in the top 10 had at least 110.
