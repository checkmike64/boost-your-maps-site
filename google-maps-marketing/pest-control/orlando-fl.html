Why does a Sanford office rarely show up in Kissimmee?

Google ranks local results by relevance, distance from the person searching, and prominence. A company based near Sanford usually shows up well in Lake Mary and Longwood and much less in Kissimmee, which sits on the far side of Orange County in Osceola.

The 417 beltway and the 408 get your trucks across the metro fast. Google still knows where your office is, so we set your service area to match the places you drive and add posts and photos from jobs in each county. Your monthly report shows which parts of the map you reach.
